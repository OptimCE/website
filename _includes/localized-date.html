{%- comment -%} Renders a date as "day month year" with a localized month name. Params: date — a date value (e.g. page.date, post.date) lang — the language code (e.g. "fr", "en", "nl", "de"); falls back to site.lang {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign ld_lang = include.lang | default: site.lang -%} {%- assign ld_t = site.data.i18n[ld_lang] -%} {%- assign ld_month_index = include.date | date: "%-m" | minus: 1 -%} {%- assign ld_month = ld_t.months[ld_month_index] -%} {{- include.date | date: "%-d" }} {{ ld_month }} {{ include.date | date: "%Y" -}}
