OptimCE is in alpha. During this phase, the hosted application is free, and installing it on your
own servers is free for good. This page sets out what is free, what could change and what will
not. It gives no price, no plan and no date: nothing of the kind has been announced to date.

## What is free today

### OptimCE Cloud, throughout the alpha

OptimCE Cloud is the hosted version, at app.optimce.be: nothing to install, nothing to maintain.
It is free throughout the alpha phase. You create an account, then your community, and you get
started.

### Self-hosting, for good

All of OptimCE's code is published under the Apache 2.0 licence: no cut-down edition, no
proprietary core. You can install it on your own infrastructure, free of charge and without
restriction, and that will not change. Both routes give access to the same features: the only
difference is who hosts the application.

### What the free offer covers

During the alpha, OptimCE Cloud is entirely free, with all its features and the annex services
included: members and meters, allocation keys with their generation and simulation, billing of
shared energy, the CWaPE administrative documents, the news board and polls. The details are on the
[energy community management software]({{ "features" | ref_url }}) page.

## What could change

A paid offer may come later, for the hosted version. If it does, alpha users will be told well in
advance. Self-hosting, for its part, will remain free.

## What it does not include

**No service guarantee when self-hosting.** On your own servers, you handle hosting, updates and
availability yourself: no service level agreement (SLA) is provided on that route.

**No price announced.** For the hosted version, nothing is promised beyond the alpha except
advance notice: no price, no plan, no service level.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Is it really free?

Yes. OptimCE Cloud, the hosted version, is free throughout the alpha phase, annex services included.
Self-hosting is free and without restriction. Both give access to the same features.

### What happens after the alpha?

A paid offer may come later for OptimCE Cloud. Alpha users will be told well in advance.
Self-hosting, for its part, will remain free.

### Can I host it myself?

Yes. All the code is published under the Apache 2.0 licence: you can install OptimCE on your own
infrastructure, free of charge and without restriction, including in a professional setting. You
then handle hosting, updates and availability yourself.

### Do I own my data?

The optimce.be privacy policy covers the website only: the application, hosted by Hostinger, is
covered by a dedicated notice inside the application. When you self-host, the data stays on your own
infrastructure.
