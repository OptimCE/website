Four topics speak first to households: the electricity bill, the grid and your grid operator, solar panels and their surplus, and energy communities, whether you want to join one or start one. The other two are mainly for project leaders and community managers: the allocation key, then the pricing and invoicing of shared electricity. Each topic opens with an overview and lists its guides.
