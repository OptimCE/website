This table holds all the data of the [“Find my DSO”]({{ 'tool-dso' | ref_url }}) tool: the electricity and gas
network operator of Belgium's 565 municipalities (19 in Brussels, 285 in Flanders, 261 in
Wallonia), with their postcodes. When several operators share a municipality, the cell names the
sections of the municipality (the former municipalities) that each one serves.
The sources and their attributions are at the [bottom of the page](#sources).
