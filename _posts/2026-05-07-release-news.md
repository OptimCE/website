The **May 2026** release of OptimCE is now available. It focuses on three things: making the platform more reliable, helping new users get up to speed faster, and opening the door to more collaboration between energy communities.

## Stability and bug fixes

This release brings a series of stability improvements and fixes reported by the community. Day-to-day operations should feel smoother, with fewer rough edges across the administration interface and the analytics backend.

## A user guide always within reach

OptimCE now ships with a complete **user guide** at [guide.optimce.be](https://guide.optimce.be). It is also accessible directly from the main navigation of this website and from the navbar inside the app, so help is always one click away. The guide focuses on how to use the app — onboarding new members, running everyday operations and getting the most out of OptimCE.

## Discover open sharing operations

The app now includes a **community directory**. Any community manager can make a sharing operation public there, and anyone with an OptimCE account — citizens, businesses, prospective members — can browse the directory to find a community near them. We hope it will help more people join an energy community and give existing communities better visibility.

The directory lives inside the application and requires an account: **[browse the community directory](https://app.optimce.be)**.

If you are looking to join an existing community, our guide [“Join an energy community in Wallonia”](/en/guides/join-energy-community-wallonia/) sets out the eligibility conditions and what to check before you sign.

As always, feedback and contributions are very welcome on [our GitHub repository](https://github.com/optimce).
